Privacy

Your information.

Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers the DED. website (isyouded.com). The DED. app has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

What you give us. When you join our waitlist or subscribe to DED.line, we collect your name and email address, plus anything else you choose to tell us in the form.

How you found us. When you sign up, we also record where you came from (for example Instagram, an email from us, or a search engine) and which page you signed up on. This helps us understand what's working.

How you use the website. We use Google Analytics to see things like which pages are visited, which buttons are clicked, roughly where in the world visitors are, and what device and browser they use. We may also use Microsoft Clarity, which shows us anonymous heatmaps and recordings of how people move around the website, so we can spot what's confusing. Clarity is set to hide anything you type. These tools use cookies and similar technology.

We don't use this information to identify you personally, and we don't use it for advertising.

How we use it

We use your information to email you about DED., invite you to beta testing, send DED.line if you've subscribed, and improve our website and product.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted services to run the website: Tally (our sign-up forms), Resend (our emails), Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Cloudflare and GitHub (hosting). Some of these store information outside New Zealand, including in the United States. We only use services that protect personal information to a standard comparable to New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of each one. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us at hello [at] isyouded [dot] com.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
